# Wrapper sound source

`wrapper-tear.mp3` and `wrapper-rustle.mp3` are trimmed and filtered excerpts from **Pack of chips, opening** (sound 1431) by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank.

Source: https://bigsoundbank.com/pack-of-chips-opening-s1431.html
Original: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/bwf-en/1431.wav
License: CC0 (public domain), confirmed on the source page October 3, 2026. Commercial use, editing and redistribution permitted.

Processing: mono 44.1 kHz MP3, high-pass filtering for handling rumble, low-pass filtering, edge fades, and reduced volume. No Triumph audio is used. The existing synthesized reveal chime and original background music are unchanged.
